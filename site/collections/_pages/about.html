杨锴埼
调色 / 摄影 / 剪辑 / DIT

2002 年生于广东，毕业于北京电影学院智能影像工程学院（原影视技术系）影视技术专业。系统学习并长期研究色彩科学、电影摄影、镜头光学、数字影院，以及 3D 立体与 HDR 制作技术。为中国电影电视技术学会（CSMPTE）会员。论文曾发表于国家级 A 类学术期刊《现代电影技术》。目前就职于北京直接调色影视科技有限公司。

我熟练使用 DaVinci Resolve、Baselight、Nuke、Avid Media Composer 等行业标准软件，并具备 Python、C++ 编程与开发能力，能够在实际项目中进行技术决策、流程优化与定制化方案设计。曾以主创或核心技术成员身份参与多部作品，作品入围国内外多个知名节展。

我是一名坦诚且对作品高度负责的合作者。我能够在剧组中灵活兼任掌机、焦点员、 后期统筹等多个角色，并可携带自有设备。我希望在尊重创作目标的前提下，以最低的成本实现最佳效果。同时，我的性格开放、幽默，善于在高强度的工作环境中为大家带来良好的氛围。

得益于扎实的工程与技术背景，我善于将创作想法转化为科学、可靠且高性价比的执行路径， 并清楚理解每一个技术选择在现场效率、后期流程与观众观感中的连锁影响。 我对影像品质近乎苛刻，也因此相信：当合作者给予我信任，我将交付一份令人惊喜的作品。

Stephen Yang Kaiqi
Colorist / Cinematographer / Editor / DIT

Born in Guangdong in 2002, Stephen Yang Kaiqi graduated from Beijing Film Academy, majoring in Digital Film Technology at the School of Intelligent Imaging Engineering (formerly the Department of Film & Television Technology). His academic training and long-term practice focus on color science, cinematography, lens optics, digital cinema systems, and Stereo 3D & HDR productions. He is a member of CSMPTE (China Society of Motion Picture and Television Engineers). His research has been published in the national Class-A journal Advanced Motion Picture Technology. He is currently working at Beijing Zhijietiaose Co., Ltd.

He is proficient with DaVinci Resolve, Baselight, Nuke, and Avid Media Composer, and has a background in Python and C++ development, enabling him to make technical decisions, optimize workflows, and design customized solutions in real-world productions. He has participated in multiple projects as a key creative or core technical contributor, with works selected for well-known festivals and showcases in China and internationally.

Stephen is an honest collaborator who takes full responsibility for the work. He is able to flexibly take on multiple on-set roles, including camera operator, focus puller, and post-production coordinator, and can bring his own equipment. He aims to achieve the best possible results at the lowest reasonable cost, while always respecting the creative goals of the project. Meanwhile, he brings an open, approachable, and humorous presence to set, helping maintain a positive atmosphere in high-intensity working environments.

With a solid foundation in engineering and imaging technology, Stephen is adept at translating creative ideas into scientifically grounded, reliable, and cost-effective execution paths. He understands how each technical choice affects the entire chain—from on-set efficiency and post-production workflows to the audience's viewing experience. Meticulous about image quality, he believes that when collaborators place their trust in him, he can deliver work that leaves a lasting impression.
